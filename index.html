05 / LEARN
⌬

Why GC content?

GC content is the percentage of bases in a DNA sequence that are guanine (G) or cytosine (C). It is a useful basic sequence-composition feature, but it does not reveal the full biological meaning of a sequence.

⚑

Model limitation

Four training sequences are far too few to establish reliable generalization. A model can appear accurate on its training examples and still perform poorly on new biological data.

✧

Genomics application

Machine learning can help researchers explore genomic patterns and prioritize sequences for further study. Any meaningful biological or clinical conclusion requires validation by qualified experts.
